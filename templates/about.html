About typosquatting-finder

Typosquatting is the registration of domain names that sound or look like a real one, to trick people into visiting the wrong site or trusting the wrong e-mail.

The service

CIRCL provides this free and public service to find typosquatted domains, so you can quickly assess whether fake domains exist that an adversary could use against you or your users.

Results can include false positives: legitimate domains that simply have a similar name. A registered look-alike is a lead to check, not a proof of abuse.

What an attacker would register

A dropped letter, two swapped, a digit that looks like a letter, another TLD. A few of the variations generated for circl.lu, with the change marked:

  • crcl.luOmission, a letter dropped
  • cirlc.luChange order, two letters swapped
  • circ1.luHomoglyph, a character that looks the same
  • ciircl.luRepetition, a letter typed twice
  • circl.comWrong TLD
  • circl.lu.comAdd TLD

How a search works

  1. Generate

    21 algorithms turn your domain into thousands of plausible typos and look-alikes.

  2. Resolve

    Each one is looked up in DNS: addresses, name servers, mail servers, and the country it is hosted in.

  3. Compare

    When a look-alike serves a website, its title, text and resources are compared with your site to spot copies.

Take the results with you

Every search has a link you can send to a colleague for one hour. Download the registered domains as JSON, the full list of variations, or a MISP event and feed ready to import.

Reading the results

Your domain
The domain you searched, shown first for comparison.
c1rcl.lu
The characters that differ from your domain are marked in red.
Your name server
Uses a name server, mail server or list you entered in the options.
Top 100k
On a public list of well-known legitimate sites (Majestic, Tranco, Moz, Google CrUX, banks, universities). Usually not a threat.
Parked
Hosted by a domain parking service. Often for sale, sometimes waiting to be used.
Catch-all mail
Accepts mail for any address, which can be used to collect mistyped e-mails.
Similarity
How close the look-alike's page is to yours, combining the similarity of their text and the share of your resources it reuses. From 50% the page is treated as a copy.

Open source

The website is an open source project. The ail-typo-squatting library that generates the variations was developed for it, and can be used on its own. Web similarity is computed with the similarius library.

Funding

This project is co-funded by the Joint Threat Analysis Network (JTAN) and CIRCL.

JTAN is an open collaboration group of European computer security incident response teams (CSIRTs) that collects, shares and analyses technical, operational and strategic threat intelligence. It addresses gaps in the threat intelligence tooling used by national CSIRTs in Europe, by strengthening individual tools and interconnecting them.
